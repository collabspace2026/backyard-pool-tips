Privacy Policy

What we collect when you visit this site, why, and what choices you have about it.

Last updated: 21 August 2026

Who this policy covers

This policy applies to backyardpooltips.com (referred to here as "the site," "we," "us"). It explains what information we collect from visitors, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

Information we collect

Information you give us directly

If you subscribe to updates, we collect your email address. If you email us, we receive whatever you send. We don't ask for or need any other personal details to run this site.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, we automatically receive some technical information when you visit — things like your browser type, general location (city/country level, from your IP address), pages viewed, and how you arrived at the site. This is typically collected via analytics tools and standard server logs.

Cookies

Cookies are small files stored by your browser. We use them (directly or via third-party tools like analytics providers) to understand how the site is used and to remember basic preferences. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings — the site will still work, though some features may behave differently.

How we use this information

  • To understand which content is useful and improve the site over time.
  • To send updates to people who've subscribed, and nothing else.
  • To keep the site secure and diagnose technical problems.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not compile personal profiles for advertising purposes beyond the standard analytics described above.

Affiliate links and third parties

This site contains links to Amazon and potentially other retailers as part of our participation in affiliate programs — see our affiliate disclosure for detail. When you click through to a retailer, that retailer's own privacy policy governs any information they collect from you. We don't receive or have access to your purchase details from these programs — only aggregated data about whether a click resulted in a sale.

Email subscribers

If you subscribe for updates, we use your email solely to send you the content you signed up for. Every email includes an unsubscribe link, and we don't share subscriber lists with third parties for their own marketing.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe from emails at any time via the link in any message.
  • Block or clear cookies through your browser's settings.
  • Contact us to ask what information we hold about you, or to request it be deleted.

Children's privacy

This site isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the site after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data — email hello@backyardpooltips.com.
